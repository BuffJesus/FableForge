# Level sweep (2026-09-16)

Every one of the 399 maps was screenshotted in the GUI from its default framing
with foliage, objects and water on (`out/sweep/sweep.txt`, generated by a one-liner
over `AlbionAtlas list`), tiled into 20 contact sheets and eyeballed; in parallel
`tools/sweep_metrics.py` exported every map with `--things` and measured how far each
placed object's origin sits above or below the terrain under it (children of mesh
dummies excluded), plus the exporter's own skip counts.

## Numbers
- 399/399 maps export; 0 missing meshes; 23 unknown-def warnings, all of them
  `OBJECT_BUILDING_DOOR_1` / `OBJECT_BUILDING_MAINDOOR_1` named by mesh dummies but
  absent from the shipped `game.bin` (genuine data gap).
- Objects more than 4 units above/below the ground are almost all legitimate: Arena
  audience on the stands, lighthouse fittings 28 m up, chandeliers, wall braziers,
  sea-wall joints sunk into the seabed, Fresco dome scene stacked 50 m above its cave.

## Found and fixed
- **Black shells over buildings** (Bowerstone Jail gatehouse, Arena sections, most
  furniture): compiled meshes carry a second, texture-less material and a full set of
  triangles on it (the physics/collision hull riding in the render list; gatehouse:
  11,955 of 18,960 triangles, `MESH_OBJECT_HOLY_START` 244 of 488). `makeMesh` now
  drops triangles on a material with no textures when the mesh has textured ones
  (`ALBION_DEBUG=1` lists what was dropped). Exports shrink ~40% in triangles.

## Seen and left alone (authentic data)
- Magenta slivers at some map edges (`NorthernWastes1_Filler_02`, `NorthernWastes3_Filler_06/07`,
  `OakVale_Sea_06`): cells painted with `INVALID_THEME_STANDIN`, which really is a
  magenta placeholder texture.
- Tiny 32x32 filler maps that look like one giant tree (`Greatwood_Filler_03`,
  `LostBay_Filler_03`, `Witchwood_Filler_24/30`): just the default framing of a map
  that is entirely canopy.
- Filler maps that are flat sand with a sheet of sea: that is what they are.

## Open
- `ExecutionTree`: two `OBJECT_HANGING_TREE_GATE_SURROUND_01` at z 43.9 sit 31 m under
  the terrain (75). Either the map has a carved passage we don't model or the gate is
  authored below a placed cliff mesh; not checked in-game.
- Cliff mapping direction is still chosen by slope, not the engine's per-layer bake.
